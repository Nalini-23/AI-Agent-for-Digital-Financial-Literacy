What the Agent Does

FinLit AI is a conversational AI assistant that retrieves verified financial knowledge and generates personalised answers using IBM Granite LLM via watsonx.ai. It supports 10 Indian languages and covers every major area of personal finance relevant to Indian users.

💳 UPI & Digital Payments

Step-by-step guides for sending money, creating a UPI ID, understanding QR codes, and safe PIN practices.

🛡️ Scam Prevention

Identifies KYC fraud, fake cashback scams, loan app scams, lottery fraud. Auto-surfaces cyber helpline 1930.

📊 Interest Rates & Loans

Explains simple vs compound interest, safe loan rate benchmarks, EMI calculation, and credit card traps.

💰 Budgeting & Savings

50/30/20 rule, emergency fund sizing, government schemes (PPF, SSY, NSC, NPS, POMIS, SCSS).

📈 Investments & SIP

Mutual fund types, SIP power of compounding, SEBI-registered advisors, ELSS tax savings.

🏦 Banking Basics

Account types, zero-balance Jan Dhan accounts, FD vs RD, DICGC deposit insurance, Banking Ombudsman rights.
